About

How GridAgent works

GridAgent answers factual questions about Germany's electricity system. It retrieves public data, calculates the result and tells you exactly which period and sources the answer is based on.

What you can ask

  • Generation by source and electricity demand, as public net generation or including industrial self-supply
  • Shares, e.g. renewables in generation or in demand
  • Day-ahead prices: averages, highs and lows, hours above or below a price, and market values (capture prices) of solar and wind
  • Cross-border flows with each neighbour, physical and commercial
  • The four transmission control areas: 50Hertz, Amprion, TenneT and TransnetBW
  • Installed capacity, the 2030 expansion targets and capacity factors
  • Official figures: CO₂ factor of the electricity mix, official renewable shares, EEG market values

Data sources

  • Energy-Charts.info by Fraunhofer ISE: public net electricity generation, 15-minute resolution (CC BY 4.0)
  • SMARD.de by Bundesnetzagentur: day-ahead prices (via Energy-Charts) and data per control area (CC BY 4.0)
  • Official statistics, quoted with their status: Umweltbundesamt, BDEW / AG Energiebilanzen, and the transmission system operators on netztransparenz.de

Method

  • Every number is calculated from the retrieved data, never estimated
  • Energy is the sum of 15-minute power values × 0.25 h
  • Shares follow Energy-Charts' definition: public net generation, excluding pumped-storage output; 50% of waste counts as renewable
  • Market value (capture price) = day-ahead price weighted by the technology's generation, as in the EEG
  • Capacity factor = generation ÷ (installed capacity at the start of the period × hours)
  • Where an official figure exists, it is shown next to the calculated one with its definition and status
  • Dates refer to German local time; “last week” means Monday to Sunday

Limits

  • Germany only, historical data only: no forecasts
  • Control areas are grid regions, not federal states
  • No investment, trading or tariff advice
  • Recent data is preliminary and can be revised by the data provider
  • At most about three months per question

GridAgent uses an AI assistant to interpret your question and present the result; the calculations themselves are done by deterministic code. Please check important figures against the original sources linked with each answer. You can ask follow-up questions; a conversation is kept in memory for 30 minutes (or until you start a new one) and then deleted. Questions are logged to monitor quality.
