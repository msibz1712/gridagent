How the chat works

Ask, and see how it was calculated.

GridAgent answers factual questions about Germany's electricity system. It retrieves public data, calculates the result in code and tells you which period, definition and sources the answer is based on.

What you can ask

  • Generation by source, demand and shares, for any period
  • Day-ahead prices: averages, highs and lows, negative hours, market values of solar and wind
  • Why a price was unusually high or low in certain hours
  • Cross-border flows and the prices of neighbouring countries
  • The four transmission control areas and weather (wind, sun)
  • Power plants by state, district and municipality, from the plant register
  • What a battery would have earned, and the cheapest hours to use power

Method

  • Every number is calculated from the retrieved data, never estimated
  • Each answer lists its steps under “How this was calculated”, with its sources and licences
  • Where an official figure exists, it is shown with its definition and status
  • Associations are described as associations, not as causes
  • Dates refer to German local time; “last week” means Monday to Sunday

Limits

  • Germany and its neighbours; past data, plus published prices and forecasts
  • No investment, trading or tariff advice
  • Recent data is preliminary and can be revised by the publishers
  • At most about three months per question

An AI assistant interprets your question and writes the answer; the calculations are done by deterministic code, and every number in an answer is checked against the calculated results. Please check important figures against the linked sources. Questions are logged to monitor quality.
